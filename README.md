## Platform Features

- **Cardiovascular Risk Prediction** — XGBoost, Random Forest, Neural Network, Logistic Regression (AUC-ROC up to 0.999)
- **Monte Carlo 5-Year Forecasting** — 600 stochastic trajectories across 4 clinical scenarios
- **Patient Retention Prediction** — dropout risk forecasting with actionable recommendations
- **Clinical NLP & Document Intelligence** — Gemini Vision reads handwritten notes and scanned PDFs
- **Medical Imaging CNN** — DenseNet-121 classifies 18 chest pathologies with Grad-CAM heatmaps
- **Pharmaco-Intelligence** — 6 drug engines grounded in ACC/AHA 2026 guidelines
- **Operational Intelligence** — bed management, staff scheduling, patient flow analytics
- **Model Performance Dashboard** — ROC curves, SHAP beeswarm, full benchmark table
